Security, Privacy & Data Minimisation Framework

How citizen identity and electronic complaint data are secured.

1. Prototype Demonstration Notice

This application is an educational, fully functional technology demonstration prototype showcasing how modern Indian Police citizen portals can seamlessly integrate digital identity verification (DigiLocker) with electronic signatures (E-Sign) for paperless complaint lodging. No real government credentials or production police databases are accessed.

2. Data Minimisation & Tokenization

In alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and IT Act 2000, unmasked biometric data or full Aadhaar numbers are never stored in plain text. Instead, cryptographic tokens and truncated phone/identity tokens are utilized.

3. Cryptographic Audit Trail & Non-Repudiation

Every e-FIR submission generates an immutable event log recording timestamp, actor, and SHA-256 digital signature hashes to prevent unauthorized modifications after submission.
